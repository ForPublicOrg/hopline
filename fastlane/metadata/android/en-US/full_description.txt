Hopline is group chat for places with no mobile signal and no WiFi: treks, camps, festivals, fairs, rallies, power cuts.

Phones link directly over Bluetooth and WiFi, and every phone passes messages along, so a message can travel down a line of hikers or across a packed ground, far beyond one phone's range.

No internet. No mobile signal. No account. No server. No hotspot to set up.

HOW IT WORKS
- One person starts a group and gets a 4-word code, like "tiger river lamp hat".
- Everyone else types the four words (or scans the QR). Done.
- Phones find each other on their own. Walk away, come back: the chat catches up.
- The group chat is encrypted with a key made from the code: anyone with the code can read it. Private chats are end-to-end encrypted - compare security codes to be sure who is who.

PHOTOS, FILES, VOICE NOTES
Tap the paperclip. Photos shrink to about 0.3 MB to hop in seconds; files up to 2 MB travel in pieces, even to phones out of range at the time. Or tap the mic for a voice note.

LOCATION - EVEN LIVE
Send where you are (GPS works with no signal) or a meeting point from coordinates or a Google Maps link. Everyone sees how far the pin is and which way. Or share live location for up to 8 hours.

REPLIES, REACTIONS, MENTIONS
Swipe to reply with a quote. Long-press to react with any emoji, double-tap for a heart. Type @ to mention someone - it always buzzes.

NAMES, MUTE, TIDY CHATS
Change your name any time, even on old messages. Rename the group for everyone. Mute or clear a chat, delete a message for yourself, reply privately or from the notification. Chats open at the first unread message.

GROUP ADMINS
Whoever starts a group is its admin and can make others admins. Admins choose who can send: everyone, or only admins.

MORE THAN ONE GROUP
Keep a group for the trek and one for the family. Switching is one tap; each keeps its own history and files.

LEAVING KEEPS THE CHAT
Leave a group and its chat, photos and files stay on your phone to read. If you're connected, the group is told. Rejoin with one tap.

HONEST TICKS
A clock while it waits for a phone in range, one tick on its way, two when phones confirm. Tap your own message to see who has it, by name.

SHARED INTERNET
If anyone in the group gets signal, everyone can use a sliver of it: the weather where you are, a text home ("I'm OK", with your location) sent by any phone with one bar of service, a look-up, or a web page as text. Ask even with no signal - the request travels with the group, and only you get the answer. Your phone helps only while "Share my internet" is on, within a daily allowance, never while roaming unless you allow it.

PAY WITHOUT INTERNET (INDIA)
No data, but a bar of signal? Hopline opens your Phone app on *99#, the banks' own UPI service, and explains each step. You type the UPI ID, amount and PIN there - Hopline never sees them. Not on Jio; up to Rs 5,000 at a time.

UPDATES, NEVER FORCED
With internet, Hopline asks GitHub for a newer version (nothing about you is sent) and fetches it on WiFi. It installs only when you tap Install.

LIMITS, STATED PLAINLY
- Each hop is Bluetooth range: about 20-40 m in the open.
- Android only. iPhones cannot join.
- A four-word code keeps out bystanders, but about a week of graphics-card time, spent once, lets someone work out any four-word code from the radio signal and read the group chat (three words: under an hour).
- Private chats have no forward secrecy; phones carrying them see who wrote to whom, and when. Chats are encrypted on the air, not on your phone.
- Phones on 2.3.1 or older can't join updated phones; private chats start fresh after the update.
- Bluetooth is flaky; links can take up to a minute to form.
- Messages are passed along for 48 hours. Chats live only on your phone: no backup.
- Leaving with nobody from the group connected tells nobody.
- 2.4 phones don't know about admins; most older groups have none.

Open source (MIT). Install it on every phone before you need it.
