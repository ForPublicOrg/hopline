Hopline is group chat for places with no mobile signal and no WiFi: treks, camps, festivals, fairs, rallies, power cuts.

Phones link to each other directly over Bluetooth and WiFi, and every phone passes messages along for the others. A message can travel down a whole line of hikers or across a packed ground, far beyond the reach of any single phone.

No internet. No mobile signal. No account. No server. No hotspot to set up.

HOW IT WORKS
- One person starts a group and gets a 3-word code, like "tiger river lamp".
- Everyone else types the three words (or scans the QR). Done.
- Phones find each other on their own. Walk away and come back - the chat catches up by itself.

PHOTOS, FILES, VOICE NOTES
Tap the paperclip. Photos are shrunk hard (about 0.3 MB) so they hop in seconds; files up to 2 MB travel in pieces and are reassembled on every phone, even ones that were out of range when you sent them. Or tap the mic for a voice note - clips are tiny and hop the same way.

LOCATION - EVEN LIVE
Send where you are (GPS works with no signal) or a meeting point from coordinates or a Google Maps link. Everyone sees how far the pin is and which way ("1.2 km away, north-east of you"). Or share live location for up to 8 hours - the People screen shows who is sharing, updating as you walk.

REPLIES, REACTIONS, MENTIONS
Swipe a message to reply with a quote. Long-press any message for a reaction bar - or + for every emoji - and double-tap to send a heart. Type @ to mention someone - being mentioned always buzzes.

NAMES, MUTE, TIDY CHATS
Change your name any time and everyone sees it, on your old messages too. Rename the group for everyone from Group info. Mute a chat, delete a message for yourself, clear a chat, reply privately, and reply straight from the notification. Chats open at the first unread message; scroll up and earlier messages load.

MORE THAN ONE GROUP
Keep a group for the trek, one for the family, one for the festival crew. Switching is one tap, and each group keeps its own history, unread counts and files.

LEAVING KEEPS THE CHAT
Leave a group and its chat, photos and files stay on your phone to read, under "Groups you left". Rejoin with one tap. Nothing is deleted until you delete it yourself.

HONEST TICKS
A clock while it waits for a phone in range, one tick when it is on its way, two ticks when phones confirm. Tap your own message to see exactly who has it, by name.

SHARED INTERNET
If anyone in the group gets signal, everyone can use a sliver of it: the weather where you are, a text home ("I'm OK", with your location) sent by any phone with even one bar of mobile service, a quick look-up, or a web page as readable text. Ask even when nobody has signal - the request travels with the group and runs when someone gets it. Answers come back only to you. Your phone helps only while "Share my internet" is on, within a daily allowance you choose, and never while roaming unless you allow it.

PAY WITHOUT INTERNET (INDIA)
No data, but a bar of phone signal? Hopline opens your Phone app on *99#, the banks' own UPI service, and explains each step. You press call and type the UPI ID, the amount and your PIN there - Hopline never sees them and can't tell if it went through. Not on Jio; up to Rs 5,000 at a time.

UPDATES, NEVER FORCED
With internet, Hopline asks GitHub whether a newer version is out - nothing about you or your group is sent - and fetches it on WiFi. It installs only when you tap Install; switch it off in Settings if you like.

LIMITS, STATED PLAINLY
- Each hop is Bluetooth range: about 20-40 m in the open.
- Android only. iPhones cannot join.
- No end-to-end encryption - anyone with the 3-word code is in the group, and every phone in it carries private chats to pass them along.
- Bluetooth is flaky; links can take up to a minute to form.
- Messages are passed along for 48 hours. Chats live only on your phone: no backup.

Open source (MIT). Install it on every phone before you need it.
