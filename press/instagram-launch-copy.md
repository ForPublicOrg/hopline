## Caption

Everyone says "there's no signal here" like it's the end of the conversation.

Hopline is a group chat that works when there's no signal, no wifi and no
router — nothing to connect to at all. Phones link straight to each other over
Bluetooth and WiFi, and every phone passes messages along for the others. So a
message can travel down a whole line of hikers, or across a packed ground, far
past the range of any single phone.

No account. No server. No SIM. Nobody's data plan.

New in 2.1 👇
📍 Send your location — GPS works with no signal — and everyone sees how far
away it is and which way: "1.2 km away, north-east of you"
📡 Or share it live for 15 minutes, an hour, or 8 hours
🎤 Voice notes, when your hands are cold and typing is a joke
↩️ Swipe to reply, long-press to react, @ to call someone out

Free. Open source. Made for treks, camps, festivals, fairs and power cuts.

Install it on every phone *before* you leave signal — that's the whole trick.

## Notes

- Every frame is the real app running on an Android device. The conversation
  shown is demo content ("Ridge trek"), not real people.
- Claims in the copy that are worth defending: distance/bearing is computed
  on-device with no network; live sharing rides existing presence beacons;
  voice notes are ~180 KB per minute; there is no account or server.
- Hopline has no end-to-end encryption — anyone with the 3-word code can read
  the group. Don't imply otherwise in comments.
